Our animals

Donkey

The donkey is a domesticated member of the horse family, easily recognized by its long ears, stocky build, and distinctive braying call. Originally adapted to arid desert environments, these hardworking animals are known for their exceptional stamina, intelligence, and sure-footed nature. Though often stereotyped as stubborn, their reluctance to move is usually a cautious self-preservation instinct when they sense potential danger.

Elephant

The elephant is the world's largest living land animal, instantly recognizable by its long prehensile trunk, large fan-like ears, and prominent ivory tusks. Highly intelligent and deeply social, these gentle giants form tight-knit family herds guided by an experienced matriarch. Native to sub-Saharan Africa and South and Southeast Asia, they play a vital ecosystem role by shaping their habitats and creating paths used by other wildlife.

Giraffe

The giraffe is the world's tallest living land mammal, instantly recognizable by its exceptionally long neck, towering legs, and distinctive spotted coat pattern. Native to the African savannas and woodlands, it uses its height alongside a long, dexterous prehensile tongue to comfortably browse leaves from high treetop foliage like acacias. Despite their massive stature, giraffes are gentle herbivores that move with a surprisingly graceful, synchronized gait across open landscapes.
